PRODUCT TRUTH

Croonbox is a live AI interview platform for hiring teams.

The employer does not manually build question after question.

The employer creates a job, adds job description, role requirements and hiring context. Croonbox then uses AI to prepare the interview structure automatically.

The candidate still takes the interview whenever they want, but once they begin, it is a live conversation with an AI interviewer.

The soul of the product is:

Employer creates job
→ Croonbox generates AI interviewer plan
→ employer previews/reshuffles/publishes
→ employer invites candidate
→ candidate opens secure link
→ candidate completes live ElevenLabs interview
→ session is recorded/transcribed
→ AI report is generated
→ employer reviews recording, transcript and report
→ employer decides Shortlist / Maybe / Reject

This core loop matters more than creating many superficial pages.

LIVE AI INTERVIEW BEHAVIOR

The interview must not be endless.

This is a major distinction.

The generated interview should usually have 4 to 6 main questions maximum.

The AI interviewer should behave like a natural professional interviewer:
- open the interview clearly
- ask one question at a time
- allow candidate to answer
- ask one useful follow-up when answer is vague
- stay within job context
- avoid random unrelated topics
- avoid protected/sensitive hiring questions
- cover the planned interview structure
- close naturally when planned questions are covered
- never give hiring decision
- never tell candidate whether they passed/failed
- never score the candidate

The AI should feel conversational and human, not like a rigid interrogation.
